## What you can do

Connect this agent connector to let your agent:

- **Update calendar list entry, acl rule, calendar** — Update the authenticated user's personal display settings for a calendar in their calendar list, such as its color, visibility, or whether it's shown
- **Ownership transfer calendar** — Transfer ownership of a secondary calendar to another user within a Google Workspace organization
- **List remove calendar from, settings, add calendar to** — Unsubscribe the authenticated user from a calendar by removing it from their calendar list
- **Event import, quick add, move** — Import a private copy of an existing event, identified by its iCalUID, into a calendar in a connected Google Calendar account
- **Get setting, colors, calendar list entry** — Retrieve a single user preference setting from a connected Google Calendar account, such as the user's timezone or week start day
- **Calendar clear** — Permanently delete all events on the authenticated user's primary Google Calendar
