## What you can do

Connect this agent connector to let your agent:

- **Update custom field, task, sequence contact status** — Update an existing custom field on contacts, accounts, or deals (opportunities) in your Apollo account
- **Get webhook result, email content, credit usage** — Retrieve the result of an asynchronous People Enrichment or Bulk People Enrichment request by its request_id, without waiting for Apollo's webhook callback
- **Task skip, complete** — Mark an existing task in your team's Apollo account as skipped, without completing it, by task ID
- **Send email** — Immediately send an existing Apollo email message that is in a drafted, scheduled, or failed state
- **Search tasks, people, news articles** — Find tasks that your team has created in Apollo, with sorting and pagination
- **List remove records from, users, lists** — Remove contacts or accounts from one or more Apollo lists, referencing the lists by name
