## What you can do

Connect this agent connector to let your agent:

- **Update teams, outlook** — Update the properties of a group chat, such as renaming its topic
- **Member teams remove chat, teams add chat, teams remove team** — Remove a member from a Microsoft Teams group chat
- **Tab teams remove channel** — Remove (unpin) a tab from a Microsoft Teams channel
- **List teams** — List the transcripts generated for a Microsoft Teams online meeting
- **Get teams, sharepoint** — Get the presence information (available, busy, away, etc.) for multiple users in a single request
- **Delete teams, outlook permanently, outlook** — Soft-delete a message in a Microsoft Teams chat
