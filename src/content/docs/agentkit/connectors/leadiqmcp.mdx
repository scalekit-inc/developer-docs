## What you can do

Connect this agent connector to let your agent:

- **Email verify prospect, verify** — Re-verify the work email already stored on a saved Prospector prospect and persist the updated status
- **Search prospects** — Search the user's saved Prospector prospects across all lists by email or full name
- **Get prospect list, prospect** — Fetch a single LeadIQ Prospector list by id, including its paginated prospect records
- **People find, enrich** — Discover new leads in LeadIQ's B2B database matching ICP criteria — filter by title, seniority, role, industry, technology stack, company size, revenue, funding, location, and hiring or promotion signals
- **Changes find job** — Discover people who recently changed jobs or were promoted, matched against ICP criteria
- **Companies find, enrich** — Discover companies matching ICP criteria in LeadIQ's B2B database — filter by industry, size, revenue, funding, technology stack, location, and more
