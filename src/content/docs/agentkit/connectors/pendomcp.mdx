## What you can do

Connect this agent connector to let your agent:

- **Queryfunnel records** — Run a unique-visitor funnel and return conversion and timing metrics for an ordered sequence of 2-3 steps
- **Objecteventbreakdown records** — Rank or trend the events/actions on one kind of business OBJECT (e.g
- **Objectanalyticstimeseries records** — Track how engagement with a business OBJECT (e.g
- **Objectanalyticsbreakdown records** — Analyze the individual business OBJECTS (e.g
- **Objectanalyticsactivecount records** — Count how many unique business OBJECTS (e.g
- **Listvisitors records** — List the visitors that match a segment and get a summary of the cohort
