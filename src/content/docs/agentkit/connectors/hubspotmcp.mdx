## What you can do

Connect this agent connector to let your agent:

- **Read campaign data** — Reads campaign data using one of three operations selected by the operation field
- **Onboarding manage** — Assess a portal's CRM onboarding status and guide the user through the next onboarding step
- **Objects manage campaign, manage crm** — Creates or updates HubSpot marketing campaigns and manages asset associations
- **Get campaign attribution reports, user details, properties** — REQUIRED FIRST STEP: before your first call, invoke tool_guidance for "get_campaign_attribution_reports" and follow it
- **Schema discover hubspot** — Searches HubSpot schema to discover available data types or look up known types directly
- **Guidance tool** — Retrieve usage instructions and guidance for one or more HubSpot MCP tools
