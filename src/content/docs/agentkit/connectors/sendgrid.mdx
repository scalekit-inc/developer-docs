## What you can do

Connect this agent connector to let your agent:

- **Update sender identity, contactdb segment, contactdb recipient** — Update an existing Sender Identity used by SendGrid's legacy Marketing Campaigns 'Campaigns' feature, by its numeric sender_id
- **Campaign unschedule, schedule** — Unschedule a Campaign in SendGrid's legacy Marketing Campaigns feature that has already been scheduled to be sent, returning it to Draft status
- **Send test campaign, campaign, test marketing email** — Send a test copy of a Campaign from SendGrid's legacy Marketing Campaigns feature to a single email address, without affecting the campaign's Draft/Scheduled status or your real recipients
- **Search contactdb recipients by field, contactdb recipient, suppression from asm group** — Search SendGrid's legacy Marketing Campaigns contact database (contactdb) for recipients matching one or more exact field=value pairs passed directly as the request's query string, e.g
- **Verification reset sender identity, reset sender** — Resend the verification email for a specific unverified Sender Identity used by SendGrid's legacy Marketing Campaigns 'Campaigns' feature, by its numeric sender_id
- **List sender identity, contactdb segment recipients, contactdb segment** — Retrieve a list of all Sender Identities configured for SendGrid's legacy Marketing Campaigns 'Campaigns' feature on this account
