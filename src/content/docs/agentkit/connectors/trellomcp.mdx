## What you can do

Connect this agent connector to let your agent:

- **Planner trello write** — Create Trello Planner calendar events and manage card-event links
- **List trello write** — Create, update, archive, or move a Trello list
- **Inbox trello write** — Write Trello Inbox cards
- **Checklist trello write** — Write Trello checklists and their check items
- **Card trello write** — Create, update, move, archive, mark Trello cards done, or manage labels on cards
- **Board trello write** — Create Trello boards
