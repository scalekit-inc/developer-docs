## What you can do

Connect this agent connector to let your agent:

- **Get time activity, tax rate, purchase** — Retrieve a single QuickBooks Online time activity by ID
- **Create time activity, purchase, vendor credit** — Create a new time activity (billable or non-billable time entry) for an employee or vendor in QuickBooks Online
- **List time activities, tax rates, report transaction** — List time activities from QuickBooks Online via the SQL-like query endpoint (entity=TimeActivity), matching the existing list-tool pattern used for other entities
- **Update sales receipt, refund receipt, purchase** — Update an existing sales receipt in QuickBooks Online
- **Balance report vendor, report customer, report trial** — Retrieve a Vendor Balance report from QuickBooks Online
- **Delete refund receipt, purchase, sales receipt** — Delete a refund receipt in QuickBooks Online
