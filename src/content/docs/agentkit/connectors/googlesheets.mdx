## What you can do

Connect this agent connector to let your agent:

- **Update spreadsheet properties, dimension properties, batch** — Update spreadsheet-level properties of a Google Sheet, such as its title, locale, or time zone
- **Whitespace trim** — Remove leading and trailing whitespace, and collapse internal whitespace to single spaces, for every cell in a range of a Google Sheet
- **Columns text to** — Split the text in a single column of a Google Sheet into multiple columns, using a delimiter such as comma, semicolon, or a custom character
- **Range sort, insert, add protected** — Sort the rows within a range in a Google Sheet by a single column, ascending or descending
- **Filter set basic, clear basic, batch clear values by data** — Create the standard 'basic filter' on a range in a Google Sheet, enabling the filter dropdown arrows in the header row
- **Search developer metadata** — Search for developer metadata entries in a Google Sheet by key, or by the sheet/row/column they are attached to
