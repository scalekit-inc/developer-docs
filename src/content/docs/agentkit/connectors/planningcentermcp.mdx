## What you can do

Connect this agent connector to let your agent:

- **Speakers publishing** — Search speakers in Planning Center Publishing
- **Series publishing** — Search sermon series in Planning Center Publishing
- **Episodes publishing** — Search episodes in Planning Center Publishing
- **Statistics publishing episode** — Church Center viewership statistics for the episodes in a single Publishing channel
- **Channels publishing** — Search sermon channels in Planning Center Publishing
- **Refunds giving** — Get the refund on a single donation in Planning Center Giving - the amount refunded, the payment processing fee returned, and when the refund was processed
