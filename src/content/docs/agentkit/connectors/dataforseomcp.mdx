## What you can do

Connect this agent connector to let your agent:

- **Serps dataforseo labs google historical** — Get historical Google SERP results for a keyword within a specified date range
- **Keywords dataforseo labs amazon related, dataforseo labs amazon ranked, dataforseo labs google related** — Get related keywords from Amazon's "Related Searches" section for a seed keyword
- **Overview dataforseo labs amazon product rank, domain analytics whois, dataforseo labs google keyword** — Get organic and paid Amazon SERP ranking data for a list of target ASINs
- **Intersections dataforseo labs amazon product kw** — Find keywords for which multiple target Amazon products (ASINs) intersect in Amazon SERP results
- **Competitors dataforseo labs amazon product, dataforseo labs google serp, backlinks** — Find Amazon products that intersect with a target ASIN in Amazon SERPs to identify product competitors
- **Search dataforseo labs amazon bulk, kw data google ads, dataforseo labs** — Get Amazon search volume data for up to 1,000 keywords in a single request
