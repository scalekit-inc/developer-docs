After first success, set up the workspace you use. Then pick a recipe for a job in your agent.

## Start with the workspace

Open [Manage environments](/how-to/environments/) next. Create development, staging, and production. Pick a US or EU region.

## Pick a recipe

Recipes are jobs in your agent. Open one, finish it, and leave.
